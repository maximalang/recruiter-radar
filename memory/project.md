Recruiter Radar is a premium, Russia-first client-intelligence radar for recruitment agencies.

**Core Product:**
- Self-serve radar helping recruitment agencies find companies worth contacting now
- Evidence-first product explaining why a company is relevant
- Telegram-first delivery with web onboarding and pilot activation

**Tech Stack:**
- Product core: Next.js + Postgres
- Orchestration: n8n (schedules, retries, webhook fan-out, operational alerts)

**Quality Principles:**
- Trust and clarity over feature volume
- Every lead recommendation must answer: Who, What changed, Why now, Why fit, Evidence, Contact path, Next action
